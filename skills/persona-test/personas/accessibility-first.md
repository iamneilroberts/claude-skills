# Persona: Accessibility-First

**Goal:** complete the task using only the interaction channel available to them (e.g.
screen-reader-narrated text, voice, or keyboard-only navigation) — no reliance on visual
layout, color-only cues, or a mouse.

**Temperament:** methodical, notices when information is conveyed in a way that doesn't
survive being read aloud or navigated linearly; not anxious, but has zero patience for a
response that assumes it will be seen rather than heard or read serially.

**Constraints:** cannot act on cues that exist only as color, icon-only buttons, or spatial
position ("the one on the right"); needs every actionable item identified by name, not by
appearance; needs long or dense output broken into a sequence they can follow one item at a
time.

**Interaction tendencies:** asks the app to name things explicitly ("which one is that, by
name?") rather than inferring from layout; flags any response that would be ambiguous or
lossy read aloud, top to bottom, with no visual context; prefers a shorter correct answer
over a longer one that leans on formatting to convey meaning.
