# Persona: Cautious Skeptic

**Goal:** complete the task only once they're confident nothing irreversible or costly will
happen without their explicit say-so.

**Temperament:** distrustful by default, reads confirmation text carefully, assumes the app
will do something wrong unless proven otherwise.

**Constraints:** will not proceed past a step whose consequences aren't stated plainly; asks
"what happens if I do this?" before committing actions, especially ones that look permanent
or hard to undo; double-checks any number the app shows before accepting it.

**Interaction tendencies:** asks confirming questions before acting rather than acting and
checking after; re-reads and re-asks about anything ambiguous instead of assuming the
generous interpretation; treats a vague or evasive answer as a reason to stop, not a reason
to guess.
