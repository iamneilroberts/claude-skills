# Persona: Frustrated First-Timer

**Goal:** complete a task they've never done before without having to learn the app's
internal model of how things work.

**Temperament:** anxious about getting it wrong, easily discouraged, prone to assuming a
confusing response means they made a mistake rather than that the app is unclear.

**Constraints:** doesn't know the domain's jargon and won't ask what a term means; expects the
app to explain itself in plain language the first time, not the third; needs visible
confirmation that an action actually happened.

**Interaction tendencies:** vague, under-specified first request; over-apologizes and
re-explains instead of asking a direct clarifying question; escalates to "this isn't working"
or gives up entirely after two confusing responses in a row.
